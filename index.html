Our history

Where we come from.

Our mission is to bring first-rate hospitality to Texas cities, and to protect our reputation on every project we take on.

Reddy Hotels & Management Group started with a small boutique hotel in Plainview, just off Interstate 27. It was the first boutique hotel the city had seen.

From that first property, the group grew into a developer of elite franchise brands, including Holiday Inn Express and Best Western Plus, and of custom boutique hotels. We have built more than twenty hotels, and in Plainview, Childress, Wills Point, Chandler and Bonham, ours was the first hotel in town.

In addition to ground-up construction, we own and manage our hotels, so that customers and communities experience hospitality at its best. Today our work extends across West Texas and the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex, into residential communities and a 20,000 square foot event center in Bonham.

Milestones

Opening doors across Texas.

The beginning

A boutique hotel in Plainview

The group’s first property, and the first boutique hotel in Plainview, sits just off Interstate 27, minutes from downtown.

Growth

Franchise brands across West Texas

The first Holiday Inn Express in Plainview, then first-in-town hotels for Childress and Chandler, established the group as a franchise developer.

2016

The first hotel in Bonham

A Holiday Inn Express opened at Highway 121 and the street the city later named GK Reddy Avenue.

The pandemic years

Wills Point opens during Covid

The city’s first hotel, a Best Western Plus, opened during the pandemic, and business has been exceptional since.

2022 onward

Breaking ground in the Metroplex

Ground broken on new hotels in Pilot Point, Farmersville and Mineola, alongside a second Bonham hotel, the Reddy Event Center and apartments in Lubbock and Bonham.

Leadership profile

GK Reddy

President & Chief Executive Officer

“There is only one boss. The guest.”

GK Reddy is a firm believer in that philosophy. He describes himself as a servant leader, on a mission to help his guests, customers, partners and the community at large achieve their goals.

With more than 40 years in the industry, and having worked every step of the way, he brings a broad range of services to every project. He has developed more than 20 projects as leader of the group, heading a team of over 50 employees and partners.

He earned his Master of Business Administration from the University of Alabama in 1984. In recognition of his contribution to the community, the City of Bonham named GK Reddy Avenue in his honor.
